How it works

  1. Install the extension and pin it to your toolbar.
  2. Visit any supported Freshworks pricing page.
  3. Click the extension icon to cycle through currencies — the badge shows the current one.
Addons with a dropdown For addon pricing that depends on an option you choose (e.g. a plan or usage tier), select the value you want first, then click the extension to convert that price too.

Generate Quote

Right-click any price on a supported pricing page — or right-click the extension icon itself — and choose Generate Quote to open a quote builder right on the page.

  1. The plan you clicked, its billing cycle, and its license cost are added as the first line item — right-clicking anywhere in a plan's card (a feature bullet, the CTA button, not just the price) resolves to that same plan. Set the number of licenses and a discount %, and it shows the Annual Cost. Month-to-month plans also get a Monthly / Quarterly / Half-yearly invoicing cadence dropdown.
  2. Use + Add addon to add any addon that's actually valid for that plan (pulled straight from the pricing page's own data), and + Add plan to quote more than one plan or product from the same page — including Custom/"Contact us" plans, which still carry a real list price behind the scenes.
  3. Toggle Direct Customer / Reseller Customer per quote. In Reseller mode, every line gets a Partner Margin % and a computed Partner Cost (annual cost minus that margin).
  4. Build multiple quotes with the quote tabs (double-click to rename, e.g. "Direct" vs "Reseller") and mark one as the customer's current subscription to see an ARR delta on every other quote, alongside the Total ARR and total discount applied.
  5. Use Download CSV or Email quote to export the quote(s) you choose.
From the toolbar icon Right-clicking the extension icon (instead of a specific price) opens the same builder empty, so you pick the plan yourself — the product is whichever pricing page you're currently on. This is an estimate only, generated from the pricing shown on the page — not a binding quote.
